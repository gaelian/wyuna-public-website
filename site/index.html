Community, sustainability, and practical support.

Wyuna Community Inc is a charity organisation located in the Australian Capital Territory. Our focus is sustainable living research and exploring alternatives to wage dependence.

We work from a long-standing interest in intentional community, self-empowerment, appropriate technology, and human relationships.

What we provide

Support grounded in community life.

Subsidised housing

Stable housing support for people connected with the community.

Mental health support

Care and guidance shaped by practical relationships and lived context.

Life skills

Career advice, life skills, and pathways toward greater self-direction.
